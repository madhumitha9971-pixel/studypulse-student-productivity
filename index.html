**Summary:** How these numbers are calculated
  • Daily progress = completed tasks ÷ total tasks × 100.
  • If the task list is empty, progress stays blank instead of a fake zero.
  • A focus session is stored only when the countdown reaches 00:00.
  • Breaks are listed, but they are not added to study time.
  • The chart uses the last 7 local days. Missing days stay at 0 min.
